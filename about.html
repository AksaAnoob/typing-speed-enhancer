About TypeMastery ⚡

This is a typing practice platform with multiple modes:

TypeMastery is a modern typing practice platform designed to help users improve their typing speed, accuracy, and consistency through engaging challenges.

The platform includes multiple game modes such as Typing Mode, Burst Mode, and Event Mode, each designed to test different aspects of typing performance.

Users can track their progress through real-time statistics like WPM (Words Per Minute), accuracy, and burst score, and compete with others through global leaderboards.

The goal of TypeMastery is simple — to make typing practice fun, competitive, and addictive while helping users steadily improve their skills.

Keep practicing. Speed is built, not given. ⚡
